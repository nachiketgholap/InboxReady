How to get the headers
  1. From the address you want to check, send a short test email to your own Gmail address.
  2. On a computer: open the email in Gmail, click the three-dot menu ⋮ next to Reply, choose Show original, then click Copy to clipboard.
  3. On a phone: the Gmail app has no Show original option. Open mail.google.com in Chrome, tap ⋮ in Chrome and tick Desktop site, open the email, then use ⋮ → Show original and select all the text and copy it.
  4. Paste everything into the box above.

Outlook.com: ⋯ → View → View message source. Yahoo Mail: ⋯ → View raw message. These are also available only on the website, not the phone apps.

Set up your domain for the inbox

Generate the DNS records that prove your emails are really from you, then follow the free warm-up plan for a new address. Add the records at the company where you bought your domain (GoDaddy, Hostinger, Namecheap, Cloudflare and others).

Record generator

Using a free @gmail.com, @outlook.com or @yahoo.com address? You can’t add DNS records, and you don’t need to: the provider signs your mail for you. Skip to the warm-up plan and send from their own website or app, not from a bulk-mail tool.

Warm-up plan for a new address or domain

New senders have no reputation. Build one slowly with people who know you and will reply.

PeriodEmails per dayWho to send to
Week 110–20Friends, colleagues, existing clients. Ask them to reply.
Week 230–50People who have emailed you before.
Week 375–100Your most active subscribers or contacts.
Week 4150–250Wider list, only people who opted in.

Habits that keep you in the inbox

  1. Send only to people who asked for your mail. Never buy lists.
  2. Remove addresses that bounce, and people who haven’t opened in 3–6 months.
  3. Ask new contacts to reply or add you to their contacts.
  4. Keep spam complaints under 0.1%. Gmail and Yahoo start filtering hard at 0.3%.
  5. Bulk senders (5,000+ a day to Gmail): SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe are required.
  6. Send bulk mail from a subdomain (news.yourdomain.com) so your main address stays clean.
  7. Keep a steady volume. Sudden spikes look like a hijacked account.
